HOW TO ADD YOUR HERO VIDEO
===========================

1. Name your video file exactly:  hero.mp4
2. Put it in this folder (assets/video/), replacing this text file.
3. Recommended specs:
   - Format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 or larger, landscape
   - Length: 10-20 seconds, looping smoothly (it will auto-repeat)
   - No audio needed - it plays muted automatically
   - File size: try to keep it under ~15MB so it loads fast on mobile data
     (you can compress with HandBrake, or ask me to compress it for you)

4. That's it - the homepage hero will automatically play the video
   once assets/video/hero.mp4 exists. Until then, it shows the
   building photo as a fallback.

Content recommendation: a short clip of children actively engaged in
class (counting, hands raised, working with number blocks) reads best
for a "Mathematics Academy" - it reinforces the numeracy brand the
moment someone lands on the page.
