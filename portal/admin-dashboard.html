Bulk Upload Results

Teachers who don't use the website can fill a spreadsheet and send it to you. You upload it here and the results are saved for every child in it. Uploading the same subject again for the same child simply updates it, so fixing a mistake is easy.

The sheet comes with every child's admission number and name already filled in. The teacher only types CA (out of 40) and Exam (out of 60) and a remark. Children only show up here after you have saved their class under "Term Details & Ratings" (or in the student import).

Term Details & Ratings

Everything on the report card that is not a subject score: class, attendance, remarks, weight, height and the A to E ratings. Positions, class high/low and averages are worked out for you. Every child needs this filled in, because it tells the system which class they belong to.
