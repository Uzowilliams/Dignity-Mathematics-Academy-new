HOW TO ADD SIGNATURES
======================

1. Scan or photograph the two signatures (Class Teacher, Head Teacher).
2. Crop each tightly around just the signature itself (no extra white space,
   no phone/scanner background) - ideally on a plain white or transparent background.
3. Save as PNG files with these EXACT names, replacing this text file:

   class-teacher.png
   head-teacher.png

4. Recommended size: around 400px wide x 150px tall (roughly 8:3 ratio),
   PNG format so it can have a transparent background if you have one.
   If the background isn't transparent, plain white works fine too since
   the report card background is white.

5. That's it - once both files exist at this exact path, they'll
   automatically appear above the signature lines on every printed
   report card. Until then, the report card just shows a blank line
   (no broken image icon).
